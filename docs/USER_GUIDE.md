# User guide

Open the address IT gives you in Edge or Chrome and sign in. Nothing needs to be installed.

## Leaders

You land on **Overview**.

- The **tiles** answer "how many": active, on track, needs attention, blocked, on hold, hired, upcoming
  deadlines, overdue actions. Click a tile to filter the table below.
- **Requires intervention** lists vacancies that are blocked, behind their agreed timeline, overdue on
  their next action or asking for support — with the reason in words.
- The **central recruitment tracker** shows, per vacancy: location, department, recruiter, hiring manager,
  current stage, status, last update, next action, action owner and deadline. Filter by recruiter,
  country, department, hiring manager or status. Click a row for the full picture.
- **Risk monitor** explains every alert: what happened, who is responsible, when it was last updated and
  what is recommended.
- **Leadership report** is the weekly report. *Save to history* keeps this week's version; *History and
  trends* shows week-by-week numbers and which vacancies are moving or stuck. Export as PDF or CSV.
- **Assistant → Leadership brief** gives the same information as short paragraphs. Each sentence lists the
  records it is based on.

## Recruiters

You land on **My workspace**.

- **Next priority** is your most urgent open action (overdue first, then due today, then by priority).
- **What I must update** lists your vacancies with no recent update, no next action or an overdue next
  action.
- **Update progress** (on any of your vacancies) takes about twenty seconds: say what changed in a
  sentence, adjust health or stage if they changed, and confirm the next action, owner and deadline. Your
  name and the date are recorded automatically. Tick *Also add this next action to the Action Centre* if
  you want it as a task.
- On a vacancy page you can **Edit milestones** (add, skip, reorder — only for that vacancy), **Edit
  record** (all fields), and add **Actions**.
- **Action centre** shows everyone's tasks grouped as overdue, due today, upcoming, waiting on others.
  Tick the circle to complete one.
- **Assistant → Reminder drafts** writes polite chasers for overdue items; copy one or open it in Outlook.
  Nothing is sent automatically.

Good habits: update each active vacancy at least once a week (the default "no recent update" threshold is
7 days), always leave a next action with an owner and a date, and record a blocker and the support you
need as soon as you are stuck — that is what puts it in front of leadership.

## Administrators

**Administration** has one tab per job:

| Tab | Use it to |
|---|---|
| Users | Add colleagues, set roles, deactivate leavers, reset passwords |
| Assign vacancies | Move one or many vacancies to another recruiter |
| Departments, countries, statuses | Maintain the lists used in forms; add your own status labels |
| Milestone templates | Define the milestone sequence for each type of recruitment process |
| Risk rules | Switch rules on or off and set thresholds (for example days without an update) |
| Permissions | Decide what each role may do |
| Integrations | Greenhouse mode and synchronisation, sign-in status, optional AI and Teams |
| Audit log | See who changed what and when |
| Data and settings | Remove demonstration data, reporting week, primary team |

**Import from Excel** (retiring the spreadsheet):

1. Upload the `.xlsx` (or `.csv`).
2. Check the suggested column mapping; correct anything marked "Likely match — check".
3. Review the preview. Rows without a job title cannot be imported. Duplicates are skipped unless you
   choose *Update the existing record* for that row.
4. Import, then open and file the validation report.

Suggested cut-over: import on a quiet day, ask each recruiter to check their vacancies in *My workspace*,
then make the Excel file read-only.

## Meaning of the statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| On track | Progressing as planned |
| Needs attention | The recruiter sees a risk or slow progress |
| Awaiting feedback | Waiting for a decision or input from someone else |
| Blocked | Cannot proceed until something is resolved |
| On hold | Paused by the business |
| Hired / Cancelled | Closed |

The recruiter sets the health; the application never guesses it.
